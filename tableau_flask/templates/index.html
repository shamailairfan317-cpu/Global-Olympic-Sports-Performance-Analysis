About This Analytics Project

This project leverages data from 120+ years of Olympic Games to build interactive visualizations that reveal how nations, athletes, and sports have evolved on the world stage. From Athens 1896 to the latest modern Games, we uncover performance patterns, medal efficiency, and demographic trends using Tableau dashboards.

The data covers over 13,000 athletes across 200+ nations and 35 sports, giving a truly global and comprehensive view of sporting excellence.

Comprehensive Data

Analysed datasets spanning 120+ years of Olympic competition, covering Summer and Winter Games from Athens 1896 to modern editions.

Global Coverage

Data from 200+ National Olympic Committees (NOCs) across every continent, enabling true global comparisons of sporting achievement.

Medal Intelligence

Deep-dive into gold, silver, and bronze medal trends — identifying which nations dominate, which sports they excel in, and how performance evolves over time.

KEY FINDINGS

Data Insights

Key discoveries from our analysis of Olympic performance data across eras, regions, and sports.

USA & USSR Dominance

The USA leads all-time medal counts, with the Soviet Union (USSR) historically competitive during the Cold War era (1952–1988).

Rising Asian Nations

China, Japan, and South Korea have seen exponential medal growth since the 1980s, reflecting increased sports investment and infrastructure.

Women's Participation

Female athlete participation grew from under 2% in 1900 to nearly 50% in recent Games, transforming the Olympic landscape.

Host Nation Advantage

Host countries typically outperform their historical averages by 30–60% in medal count, driven by home crowd support and familiarity.

Sport-Specific Efficiency

Small nations maximise medals through niche sports — e.g., Jamaica in sprinting and Kenya in long-distance running.

Regional Patterns

Europe consistently holds the highest total medal share. Africa and Oceania show strong per-capita efficiency in select disciplines.
